Last Light: the swarm, top down

The game's own swarm (src/swarm.ts, inlined at build), drawn as dots. Every rat follows one rule: run straight at the light. It will not step in, and it cannot push through the rat in front, so it slides sideways to wherever there is room. The pushing is Helbing's social-force crowd. The light moves no rat: it walks, and they get out of its way, or don't.

Colours

By fear: a rat warms from grey to amber the longer it stands the light's edge, and cools once it backs off.

calm burning, toward its limit

By gait: each rat's feet are picked from how fast it really moved over the last third of a second, never from what it wants.

idle: stuck in the crush walking running

Fear

Off, the light never burns: no rat flinches, and the front holds the edge.
