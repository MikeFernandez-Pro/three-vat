Rats round walls

The game's own swarm (src/swarm.ts, inlined at build) twice, same rats, same light. Left, as it is: every rat aims straight at the holder and slides along any wall in the way. Right, one change: a rat out of the light's clear sight takes the way a flow field gives, the shortest way round the walls, and its distance along it for its notice. Everything else, the crowd, the fear, the light's edge, is the same code.

The field: the ground in 0.25 m cells; cells the light sees clearly hold their straight distance, the rest are filled outward round the walls (Dijkstra), and a rat steers down the slope. Rebuilt when the light moves a cell.
