# The braziers: an art brief

A brief for ChatGPT, which draws **four separate pictures of one brazier**
(front, left side, right side and back), and then for Tripo3D, which turns
those four pictures into a 3D model. Section 4 holds the prompts to paste, in
order, in **one chat**. **One model** is made, and the game places it three
times. The model is the **brazier only**: the fire is a game effect, so the
pictures show no flames.

**The reference:** `exports/brazier-reference.png` (git-ignored), a
stylized stone pedestal carrying an iron bowl in a crown-shaped cage. Its
shapes are the ones wanted; its colours are not. Attach it to prompt 1.

---

## 1. What it is in the game

*Last Light* is a small horror game. A kid of about 8 (1.2 m tall, with an
orange pumpkin head and a burning stick for a torch) has to escape a sewer
full of rats. Light holds the rats off, and his torch burns down.

Three braziers burn in the sewer, each a safe place and a place to relight
the torch, holding the stick into the bowl over its rim:

- **the gas tunnel's mouth:** the last light before a long dark run past the
  gas jets;
- **the landing:** where he climbs out of the canal, on the cistern's
  threshold;
- **the cistern's middle:** among its four pillars, lighting the plates
  round it.

What the brazier needs to read as:

- **a light someone set here:** a small stone pedestal and an iron fire
  bowl, smaller and plainer than the hub's great fire basin;
- **the same masonry as the cistern:** the sage, blue-green stone of the
  pillars, so the brazier among them belongs to them;
- **easy to read from far off:** the iron crown on top gives it a silhouette
  the player spots in the dark, before the fire's light reaches him.

## 2. The brazier (in metres; the kid is 1.2 m)

A **simple** prop with only four parts, as in the reference. Nothing else.

1. **The base:** a low, square stone slab, about **0.7 m wide and 0.15 m
   tall**, its top edges cut on a broad bevel, with one crack across it.
2. **The shaft:** a square stone pillar, about **0.4 m wide and 0.5 m
   tall**, built of big soft-edged blocks laid unevenly, a little wider at
   its foot. A shallow **square recessed panel**, plain and empty, on the
   **front face only**: the one detail that tells the front from the sides
   and back.
3. **The cap:** a square stone block on top, about **0.52 m wide and 0.12 m
   tall**, its edges bevelled like the base's, overhanging the shaft a
   little.
4. **The fire bowl:** a round, shallow blackened iron bowl, about **0.55 m
   across**, standing on **four short iron legs** on the cap. Round it, a
   **crown-shaped cage** of flat iron straps: a band round the bowl's middle,
   a band a little above its rim, and **six flat uprights** rising through
   both bands, each curling **outward** at its tip like a claw. Inside, a low
   heap of black coal and charred wood, showing just below the rim. It is
   cold: no flames, no glow.

The bowl's rim stands at about **1 m**, the kid's chest, so he can hold his
stick over it; the uprights' tips reach about **1.15 m**. The whole model is
about **0.7 m wide and 1.15 m tall**.

**The damp:** the sewer's wet has darkened the stone's foot, with a pale
crust of limescale, a little moss in the base's crack, and dark stains
running down from the cap. The iron is sooty toward the rim, with teal-green
verdigris at the straps' edges and rivets.

## 3. Style and colour

- **Style:** a stylized, hand-painted game prop, chunky and slightly
  exaggerated, like the reference, Dofus or *Penguins of Madagascar* concept
  art. Big simple shapes, big stone blocks with soft edges, few details,
  clean silhouette.
- **Colours** (not the reference's):
  - **stone:** pale blue-green and sage, as the cistern's pillars, darker and
    greener at the foot;
  - **bowl and cage:** blackened iron, sooty toward the rim, teal-green
    verdigris at the edges;
  - **fuel:** black coal and charred wood;
  - a pale crust of limescale at the foot.
- **Leave out:** flames, glow, smoke, orange, rust, copper, text, carvings in
  the panel, skulls, and any ornaments.

## 4. The prompts

Paste them one after the other in **the same chat**, so ChatGPT keeps the
same brazier from picture to picture. **Attach the reference picture to
prompt 1.**

### Prompt 1: the front view, and how the four pictures work

> I need reference pictures of one 3D game prop so that a 3D tool (Tripo3D)
> can rebuild it. I will ask you for **four separate pictures, one at a time,
> each in its own image**: the FRONT, the LEFT side, the RIGHT side and the
> BACK. Please do NOT put several views in one image, and do not make a sheet
> or a grid.
>
> The rules for all four pictures:
> - exactly the same object, same size and same position in the frame, centred
>   and fully visible from the floor to the top, nothing cropped;
> - a perfectly straight-on orthographic view, with no perspective, no
>   three-quarter angle, and the camera at the object's mid-height;
> - a plain light-grey background, flat even lighting, no cast shadows, no
>   environment, no other objects, no text or labels.
>
> The attached picture shows the kind of prop I want: follow its **shapes**
> closely, but **not its colours**, and not its three-quarter angle.
>
> The prop: a brazier from an old underground sewer, in a stylized
> hand-painted game-asset style (like the attached picture, or Dofus), chunky,
> with big simple shapes and few details. It is about 1.15 m tall and has
> only four parts:
> 1. a low square stone base slab, about 0.7 m wide and 0.15 m tall, its top
>    edges cut on a broad bevel, with one crack across it;
> 2. a square stone shaft, about 0.4 m wide and 0.5 m tall, of big
>    soft-edged blocks laid unevenly, a little wider at its foot, with a
>    shallow square recessed panel, plain and EMPTY, on the FRONT face only;
> 3. a square stone cap block on top, its edges bevelled, overhanging the
>    shaft a little;
> 4. on the cap, a round shallow blackened iron fire bowl on four short legs,
>    held in a crown-shaped cage of flat iron straps: one band round the
>    bowl's middle, one band a little above its rim, and six flat uprights
>    rising through both bands, each curling OUTWARD at its tip like a claw.
>    Inside, a low heap of black coal and charred wood. It is cold: no flames,
>    no glow, no smoke.
>
> The stone is damp: darker at the foot, a pale limescale crust, a little
> moss in the crack, dark stains running down from the cap. The iron is
> sooty toward the rim, with teal-green verdigris on the straps' edges.
>
> Colours: pale blue-green and sage stone, blackened iron with teal-green
> verdigris, black coal. No fire, no orange, no rust, no copper, nothing
> carved in the panel, no ornaments.
>
> Now draw **picture 1 of 4: the FRONT view**, looking straight at the face
> with the empty panel, the panel in the middle of the shaft.

### Prompt 2: the left side

> Now **picture 2 of 4: the LEFT side view** of exactly the same brazier.
> The camera has turned 90° to the brazier's left. This face is plain
> blocks, with no panel; the front panel is hidden, at the LEFT edge of the
> shaft. The round bowl and its six claws look the same as from the front,
> turned a little. Keep the same size, position, background and lighting as
> picture 1, straight-on and orthographic.

### Prompt 3: the right side

> Now **picture 3 of 4: the RIGHT side view** of exactly the same brazier.
> The camera has turned 90° to the brazier's right. Plain blocks, no panel;
> the front panel is hidden, at the RIGHT edge of the shaft. Keep everything
> else identical to picture 1.

### Prompt 4: the back

> Now **picture 4 of 4: the BACK view** of exactly the same brazier, the
> camera turned 180° from the front. Plain blocks, no panel. Keep everything
> else identical to picture 1.

**If a picture drifts** (flames or glow appearing, the reference's brown or
grey colours, copper, a carving in the panel, the panel on another face, the
cage with another number of uprights or claws curling inward, an angle
instead of straight on, two views in one image), say exactly what is wrong
and ask for that picture again. Tripo3D needs the four views to match.

## 5. After Tripo3D (Blender)

- **The pivot** goes at the middle of the base, on the floor.
- **The fire** comes from the game, not from the model: flames, embers and
  light sit over the coal, at about 1 m.
- **One model, three braziers:** use linked duplicates, each turned so its
  panel faces the way the kid comes from, or a different way, so the three
  don't look copied.
- **The guide (`exports/sewers.glb`):** each brazier replaces its
  placeholder (an iron cylinder 0.7 m across and 0.9 m tall with its flame
  on top), keeping its name and its light's reach:
  - `gas mouth` at (-10.45, -11.9), reach 1.3 m;
  - `landing` at (0.04, -42.66), reach 2.4 m;
  - `symbol door` at (4.62, -50.86), the cistern's middle among the pillars,
    reach 1.3 m.
- The base stays within the placeholder's 0.7 m, so the model takes no more
  floor than the plan gives it.
