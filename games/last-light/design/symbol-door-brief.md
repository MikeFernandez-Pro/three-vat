# The cistern's symbol door: an art brief

A brief for ChatGPT, which draws **four separate pictures of one door**
(front, left side, right side and back), and then for Tripo3D, which turns
those four pictures into a 3D model. Section 4 holds the prompts to paste, in
order, in **one chat**. The pictures show the door **shut**.

The door carries **no symbols**. The puzzle's symbols are on the plates and
the buttons, and are added later there.

---

## 1. What it is in the game

*Last Light* is a small horror game. A kid of about 8 (1.2 m tall, with an
orange pumpkin head and a burning stick for a torch) has to escape a sewer
full of rats.

Near the end he reaches the **cistern**: an old round water tank, 14 m across,
long since drained and full of rats. Four plates in its floor each show a
numeral and a symbol. In the cistern's north wall is the **symbol door**, the
way out, with a row of four button posts in front of it. Pressed in the
plates' order, the buttons open it: the gate **rises straight up into the
wall**, and the cistern's rats pour after him down the last gallery to the
ladder.

What the door needs to read as:

- **the cistern's old outlet:** a wide, low mouth in the tank's wall, the
  one the water drained through. Its top sits right on the water line, at
  1.6 m, the same line as on the pillars;
- **a sluice gate:** a heavy steel gate that slides up and down in iron
  grooves, held by two chains that vanish into the stone above;
- **locked by the buttons:** iron rods come up out of the floor, from the
  buttons' side, into lock boxes beside the gate, so the player sees the
  buttons work it.

## 2. The door (in metres; the kid is 1.2 m)

A **simple** prop with only four parts. Nothing else.

1. **The wall:** a flat stretch of the cistern's wall, **4.6 m wide, 3.5 m
   tall and 0.6 m deep**, of big soft-edged stone blocks, the same masonry as
   the pillars. The gate rises into it, out of sight, so it must look thick
   and solid.
2. **The mouth:** a wide, low rectangular opening in its foot, **3.6 m wide
   and 1.6 m tall**. Over it lies a heavy stone **lintel**, one long block
   about 0.4 m tall, with a narrow slot along its underside where the gate
   goes up. Down either side runs an **iron guide groove**, set into the
   stone, in which the gate slides. A stone sill across the floor.
3. **The gate:** a heavy riveted steel plate, about **3.8 m wide and 1.75 m
   tall**, filling the mouth, its edges in the grooves and its top in the
   lintel's slot. Three horizontal stiffening ribs across its front, big
   rivets. Two lifting lugs at its top corners, each with a heavy **chain**
   rising into the slot in the lintel.
4. **The locks:** a small riveted steel **lock box** on each side of the
   mouth, low on the wall's face, about 0.3 m wide and 0.5 m tall, shooting
   a thick bolt into the gate's edge. Into each box, **two iron rods** come
   up out of the floor in front of the wall: four rods in all, one from each
   button. The lock boxes and rods are on the **front only**: they tell the
   front from the back.

**The water line:** a clear horizontal line across the whole wall at
**1.6 m**, level with the mouth's top. Below it, the stone is darker,
stained green-brown, with a pale crust of limescale and a little moss at the
foot. Above it, the stone is paler and drier. The gate below it is stained
and crusted too.

The whole model is about **4.6 m wide, 3.5 m tall and 0.6 m deep**, with the
rods standing about 0.3 m out in front.

## 3. Style and colour

- **Style:** a stylized, hand-painted game prop, chunky and slightly
  exaggerated, like Dofus or *Penguins of Madagascar* concept art. Big simple
  shapes, big stone blocks with soft edges, big rivets, few details, clean
  silhouette. The same family as the pillars and the huge door.
- **Colours:**
  - **stone:** pale blue-green and sage, darker and greener below the water
    line;
  - **gate, grooves, locks, rods and chains:** dark blackened steel, wet and
    grimy, with teal-green verdigris round the rivets and in the worn places;
  - a pale crust of limescale below the water line.
- **Leave out:** orange, rust, gold, handwheels, text, numbers, symbols,
  emblems, carvings, and any ornaments.

## 4. The prompts

Paste them one after the other in **the same chat**, so ChatGPT keeps the
same door from picture to picture.

### Prompt 1: the front view, and how the four pictures work

> I need reference pictures of one 3D game prop so that a 3D tool (Tripo3D)
> can rebuild it. I will ask you for **four separate pictures, one at a time,
> each in its own image**: the FRONT, the LEFT side, the RIGHT side and the
> BACK. Please do NOT put several views in one image, and do not make a sheet
> or a grid.
>
> The rules for all four pictures:
> - exactly the same object, same size and same position in the frame, centred
>   and fully visible, nothing cropped;
> - a perfectly straight-on orthographic view, with no perspective, no
>   three-quarter angle, and the camera at the object's mid-height;
> - a plain light-grey background, flat even lighting, no cast shadows, no
>   environment, no other objects, no text or labels.
>
> The prop: the outlet door of an old drained underground water cistern, in
> a stylized hand-painted game-asset style (like Dofus), chunky, with big
> simple shapes and few details. It is a section of wall with a sluice gate,
> and has only four parts:
> 1. a flat stretch of thick stone wall, 4.6 m wide, 3.5 m tall and 0.6 m
>    deep, of big soft-edged stone blocks;
> 2. in its foot, a WIDE, LOW rectangular opening, 3.6 m wide and only 1.6 m
>    tall, under one long heavy stone lintel with a narrow slot along its
>    underside, an iron guide groove down each side, and a stone sill;
> 3. filling the opening, SHUT, a heavy riveted steel sluice gate with three
>    horizontal ribs across its front, its edges in the grooves, its top in
>    the lintel's slot; two lifting lugs at its top corners, each with a
>    heavy chain rising into the slot;
> 4. on the FRONT only, a small riveted steel lock box low on the wall on
>    each side of the opening, shooting a thick bolt into the gate's edge,
>    with two iron rods coming up out of the floor into each box.
>
> Across the whole wall at 1.6 m, level with the opening's top, runs a clear
> old water line. Below it, the stone is darker, stained green-brown, with a
> pale limescale crust and a little moss at the foot; the gate is stained
> and crusted too. Above it, the stone is paler and drier.
>
> Colours: pale blue-green and sage stone, dark blackened wet steel with
> teal-green verdigris round the rivets. No orange, no rust, no gold, no
> handwheels, no symbols, no emblems, no carvings, no text.
>
> Now draw **picture 1 of 4: the FRONT view**, looking straight at the gate,
> the opening centred at the bottom of the wall, a lock box either side.

### Prompt 2: the left side

> Now **picture 2 of 4: the LEFT side view** of exactly the same door. The
> camera has turned 90° to the door's left, so the wall is seen end-on: a
> narrow upright slab of stone 0.6 m deep and 3.5 m tall. The front is at
> the LEFT: the lock box and its two rods stand out from the wall's foot
> toward the left. The water line runs round the end of the wall at the same
> 1.6 m. Keep the same size, position, background and lighting as picture 1,
> straight-on and orthographic.

### Prompt 3: the right side

> Now **picture 3 of 4: the RIGHT side view** of exactly the same door. The
> wall is seen end-on from its right; the front is at the RIGHT, the lock
> box and its two rods standing out from the wall's foot toward the right.
> Keep everything else identical to picture 1.

### Prompt 4: the back

> Now **picture 4 of 4: the BACK view** of exactly the same door, the camera
> turned 180° from the front, looking from the dark gallery behind it: the
> plain back of the stone wall, the same low wide opening, and the plain
> riveted back of the shut gate in its grooves. No lock boxes, no rods and
> no ribs on this side. The water line is here too, at the same 1.6 m. Keep
> everything else identical to picture 1.

**If a picture drifts** (the opening taller than it is wide, or arched, the
gate swinging on hinges, a symbol or emblem anywhere, a handwheel, the lock
boxes on the back, the water line at another height, an angle instead of
straight on, two views in one image), say exactly what is wrong and ask for
that picture again. Tripo3D needs the four views to match.

## 5. After Tripo3D (Blender)

- **The pivot** goes at the middle of the wall's foot, on the floor.
- **Moving parts:** Tripo3D gives back one fused mesh. Cut apart in Blender:
  - **the gate with its chains:** its pivot at the middle of its foot. It
    rises **1.6 m straight up**, into the wall, its top stopping at about
    3.35 m, under the wall's 3.5 m top. Hollow a slot in the wall above the
    lintel, about 0.15 m deep and 1.8 m tall, so the raised gate is hidden;
  - **the two bolts:** each slides 0.1 m back into its lock box before the
    gate moves.
- **The guide (`exports/sewers.glb`):**
  - The door replaces the `symbol door` placeholder, a slab from (2.3,
    -57.75) to (6.1, -57.75). Centre the wall on that line, at x 4.2, its
    front facing south (+z), into the cistern.
  - The mouth matches the last gallery behind it, 3.6 m wide from x 2.4 to
    6.0.
  - The four buttons stand 1.3 m in front, at x 2.7, 3.7, 4.7 and 5.7,
    z -56.45: the rods come up between them and the wall, so leave them
    clear.
  - The wall section is flat; the cistern's round wall meets it either side.
