Last Light: the level

Started 2026-10-10. Beats 1 to 3 are built in the 2D prototype. Beats 3b and 4 are redrawn below from the world (2026-10-10), not yet rebuilt in 2D: the prototype's quarry stairs are out of date. About a 5 min run. The player should finish feeling they survived. decided is what you chose. proposed is a draft to react to. open still needs a decision. reserve is kept for later.

The world

decided Where he's going: home, a manor. decided The forest stays a forest, full of rats. decided Built from simple shapes: this is a three.js game, not a AAA one, and the modelling budget is small. Every structure is boxes, cylinders and a few rocks.

proposed The child lives at the manor, and is lost in its forest on Halloween night, in pyjamas with a pumpkin head. open Why he's out there.

proposed The storm breaks over the forest tonight, and it explains everything broken: the lightning that strikes the first tree, the cracked tree it fells, the pine blown down across the ravine, and the rain.

proposed The road home: the manor's cart road runs out of the forest, over the river by its bridge, through the estate wall by its gate, and up the wooded hill to the ravine's footbridge, then on to the park, the garden, the door. Every built thing the child meets is on that road, and was put there by the people of the manor.

proposed The gamekeeper was on his night round. He carried fire in the brazier cart, lit the braziers along the hill path and the torch under the gate's arch, and left the cart on the bridge. The rats got him by the gate, and the key is on him.

decided Why the child follows the road: the dark belongs to the rats, and the only lights in the forest are the ones people lit along it. He never goes a way because a wall says so; he goes where the light is.

The references: A Plague Tale: Innocence (light against the swarm, walking the lit path between the rats), Dark Souls (a landmark seen long before it's reached), Shadow of Mordor, The Witcher 3 (places with a history, a body that tells what happened), No Rest for the Wicked (a handcrafted world read from above).

Rules of the level

  • decided Puzzles are simple. The challenge is choosing right and managing the light.
  • decided Linear, but the player shouldn't notice: most of the time there are only one or two obvious directions.
  • decided The space feels open. Trees, rocks and fallen trunks limit it in the wild; built things stand only where the manor's people built them.
  • decided Every structure has a reason: who built it, where it leads, and why it's in the state it's in tonight. Every way the child can't go has a reason he can see.
  • decided No fuel stock. The torch burns for a set time, and refuels at any flame. What limits the player is how far apart the flames are and how fast the torch burns.
  • decided The flame dims steadily from the moment it's lit, so how much time is left is always readable. The reach still holds full for three quarters of the burn, then shrinks over the last quarter, as today.
  • decided Timings are tuned to the last second, as in Outlast 2: one more second would have been too late.
  • decided Every mechanic is shown before it's tested. A key is seen before it's found.
  • decided The nearest flame as the crow flies is a trap: it's further on foot. Read the ground, not the glow.
  • dropped Streams, anywhere. open What cuts the trap flame off in beat 5.
  • decided Every obstacle comes first in a safe version: the first crawl is under a burning trunk, inside its light.
  • decided Rats are the only danger. They're never something to solve.
  • decided Events are scripted by the level. Short scripted text lines guide the player.
  • dropped Wind, gusts, shelter behind rocks, rat nests, luring.

That's for a player who walks straight. One who stops when the flame disappears behind the rock arrives with 0 to 3 s left. Here's the Outlast trick: the reach shrinks only in the last 15 m, with the flame already in view, so 4 real seconds feel like the last one. If play-testers die here, move the faint flame closer. That's a change to the level data, not to the rules.

The doubt is built in. The flame is seen at P1, then vanishes behind the tall rock at P2, just when the light has visibly dimmed. It comes back at P3, close.

What the 2D demo needs for these two beats

  • An opening with control locked, scripted.
  • A light that switches on at a set time: the two strikes. It's the fragile flame's script in reverse.
  • A wall that appears at a set time and can only be passed by crawling: the falling trunk.
  • Crawl: C as a toggle, left Ctrl held, slower: decided 1 m/s, half the walk.
  • The torch starts unlit, and lights at a flame the way it refuels.
  • A low obstacle that blocks the way but not the view: the thicket.
  • Text lines, each set off by a trigger.
  • The flame dimming steadily. In 2D, the torch's glow fades.
  • The designer overlay: seconds of light left at each route point.

The gameplay values then stay the same because it's the same code, not copied numbers. The level is written once as data, and the 3D page loads that same data later.

proposed The 2D view is dark outside the lights, and a distant flame still shows as a glow. That tests sightlines the way 3D will see them.

proposed A designer overlay shows the seconds of light left on arrival at each flame on the intended route. That's how the "last second" moments get tuned.

Open questions

  • open The safe zone: could be the glow seen from the burning tree. Decide once the level's size is known.
  • open Fog in 3D: today it hides everything far away. The beam's view of the field of flames, and any distant goal, need a new rule, for example: fog hides the ground and the rats, but flames show through it as glows.
  • open Later: whether the beam's far end is dangerous, depending on how fast the player crossed.
  • open The dark run's speed. Today the child walks at 2 m/s and the fastest rats run at 2.3 m/s, so they gain on a walking child. The run speed and the distance to the safe zone set how close they get; that's where the last second is tuned. Crawling under trunks is where they gain most.
