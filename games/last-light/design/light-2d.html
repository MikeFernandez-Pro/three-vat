Rays and rats, top down

The game's own swarm (swarm.ts, inlined in this file). The torch casts 512 rays; the rats read the same table to know where the light stops. Click or drag to lead the torch, or use WASD / the arrow keys.

The table everything reads, live: one row per light, 512 columns (the angle), bright = far, dark = a wall close by. Top row the torch, bottom row the brazier.
